# Ankit & Namitha — Wedding Invitation

A mobile-first, premium wedding invitation website for Ankit & Namitha.

## Included
- Full-screen photo hero with editorial wedding styling
- Animated preloader and navigation drawer
- Invitation message
- Live countdown to 4 December 2026 (IST)
- Scratch-to-reveal wedding dates
- Story section using the supplied couple photograph
- 3 Dec 2026 — Ring Ceremony & Haldi
- 4 Dec 2026 — Wedding
- Google Calendar button
- Venue section with a styled map card and Google Maps link
- Exact supplied coordinates: 13°41′44.5″N, 75°28′10.0″E
- 9 supplied couple photographs in a responsive masonry-style gallery
- Tap/click gallery lightbox
- Browser-local Wall of Wishes
- Mobile-first responsive design
- No SatyaNarayan Puja (removed as requested)
